About

Dr Doudou Zhang is an early-career researcher working on solar-driven (photo)electrochemical catalysis, with the aim of turning sunlight, water and low-cost materials into chemical fuels.

She joined Macquarie University in July 2024 as a Macquarie University Research Fellow, after five years as a research fellow at the Australian National University. Her PhD in materials science is from Shaanxi Normal University.
